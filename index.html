Get Jarvis in 4 steps

Click a number to check it off. Takes about 10 minutes, mostly downloading.

1

Install Ollama

Ollama runs the AI on your PC. It’s free.

Get Ollama ↗ ollama.com
2

Download the AI model

Open Command Prompt and run this once (about 5 GB):

ollama pull llama3.1:8b
3

Download Jarvis

Version 1.0.0 for Windows, a single .exe file.

Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because the app isn’t code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
4

Run it and say “Jarvis”

Open Jarvis.exe, pick your microphone, press START, and try “Jarvis, open Notepad.”

Heads up: speech recognition uses Google’s free speech service, so your mic audio is sent there while Jarvis is on. Press STOP when you don’t want it listening.
